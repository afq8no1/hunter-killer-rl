How it works

Hunters share one Q-table (tabular Q-learning, α=0.15, γ=0.95, ε-greedy). Each hunter sees only within 6 cells and line-of-sight. Its state is: direction and distance to the team's last-known target, whether it sees you now, which neighbouring cells are walls, and its last move. Rewards: +10 for the kill, small bonuses for spotting and closing in, a tiny penalty per step.

The assassin is a scripted evader during training (flees what it sees, wanders otherwise). Hit Play to control it yourself with arrows/WASD or swipe (tap to stop). Hunters keep learning from you. Survive 250 ticks to win. Try Reset brain to feel the difference between untrained and trained hunters.
